Sunbed Safety Information

Please read the following information carefully before using the sunbed facilities.

Important Safety Information

  • Sunbeds are strictly for persons aged 18 years or over.
  • Always wear appropriate eye protection while using a sunbed.
  • Do not exceed the recommended session times.
  • Allow at least 24 hours between tanning sessions.
  • Do not use a sunbed if you are taking medication that may increase sensitivity to UV light.
  • Do not use a sunbed if you have a history of skin cancer.
  • Do not use a sunbed if you are sunburnt.
  • If you experience irritation or discomfort, stop use immediately.

Skin Type Guidance

Sensitive: Burns very easily and rarely tans.

Fair: Usually burns before tanning.

Medium: May burn occasionally and tans gradually.

Dark: Rarely burns and tans easily.

Client Declaration

By using the salon's sunbed facilities you confirm that:

  • You are at least 18 years old.
  • You have read and understood this safety information.
  • You will follow staff guidance regarding session lengths.
  • You understand that excessive UV exposure may increase health risks.
